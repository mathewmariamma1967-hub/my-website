Major research project

Development of Information System on E-Portfolio Based Knowledge Generation and Sharing for Teacher Professional Development – UGC, Dec 2012 – Dec 2014, Rs 7,26,100, Principal Investigator. The system (teacherseportfolio.com) lets student teachers upload their best work, assessed with rubrics and visible to prospective recruiters. It was extended to several teacher training institutions in Kerala and is described as the first of its kind in Indian teacher education.
